liquid‑tab‑bar
a bottom nav that sags under your finger.

The bar's surface is a real membrane — it dents downward under a ball that follows your pointer and settles onto the tab you release over. No images, no blur hacks, no framework. Drop in one script and one stylesheet.

★ Star on GitHub How to use it
Grab the ball and drag it along the bar below ↓

Home

Tap a tab, or drag the ball. Active state tracks the URL hash — here it swaps these panels, in a real app it follows your router.

MembraneA computed Gaussian dip, not a blur filter.
FollowrequestAnimationFrame easing with liquid lag.
SettleRelease and the ball snaps to the nearest tab.

Explore

Each tab carries its own accent colour. The ball and its glow tint to whichever tab it's resting on — try dragging slowly across all five.

ThemingEvery colour is a --ltb-* CSS variable.
Icons14 built in; or drop your own inline SVG.
Light modeAdd data-theme="light".

Create

Progressive enhancement: you write plain, accessible <a> links. The script upgrades them in place, so it still works and still reads correctly if the JS never loads.

AccessibleReal links, aria-current, focus rings.
KeyboardTab to focus, Enter to navigate.
PointerMouse, touch and pen via Pointer Events.

Saved

Reduced-motion aware — if the visitor asks their OS for less motion, the ball jumps straight to the tab instead of easing.

RespectfulHonours prefers-reduced-motion.
ResponsiveStretches to its container, caps at 480px.
Safe-areaClears the iOS home indicator.

Profile

One LiquidTabBar.mount() call, or just the data-liquid-tab-bar attribute for auto-init. That's the whole API.

~6KBJS + CSS, gzipped. No build step.
No depsVanilla JS. Works with any framework or none.
MITUse it anywhere, commercial included.

Drop it in

<!-- 1. the markup: plain, accessible links -->
<nav class="liquid-tab-bar" data-liquid-tab-bar>
  <a href="#home"    data-icon="home">Home</a>
  <a href="#explore" data-icon="compass">Explore</a>
  <a href="#create"  data-icon="grid">Create</a>
  <a href="#saved"   data-icon="heart">Saved</a>
  <a href="#profile" data-icon="user">Profile</a>
</nav>

<!-- 2. the files (auto-initialises on load) -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="liquid-tab-bar.css">
<script src="liquid-tab-bar.js"></script>
